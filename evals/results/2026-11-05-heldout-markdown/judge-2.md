# Anonymous visual review

Preferred: **A**, narrowly. A scores 18/24; B scores 17/24. The deciding evidence is the usable writing area and explicit writing/preview navigation on the 390×844 phone. This is a task-fit judgment, not a preference for either palette or panel style.

## Scores and evidence

The six dimensions below use 0–4, where 3 means clear and coherent and 4 requires evidence of exceptional execution.

1. **Recognition and scanning — A 3, B 3.** Both clearly identify Markdown Editor, distinguish source from rendered text, and retain named toolbar actions. A's “Read preview” and “Back to editor” make the destination especially explicit. B's source/preview headers, icons and Live indicator make the relationship immediately visible on both sizes. A's small all-caps panel captions and B's very small Live/.md annotations are secondary information rather than strong scan anchors.

2. **Hierarchy, grouping, type and spacing — A 3, B 3.** Both provide distinct document headings, comfortable preview body leading, recognizable lists, blockquotes, tables and code blocks in the populated desktop and reading screenshots. B separates file actions on the left from view controls on the right and groups each workspace within a clear header and boundary. A uses simpler continuous surfaces and clear column headings. Neither shows a major hierarchy failure; neither provides enough evidence for an exceptional score across the full task.

3. **Content and actions supporting the task — A 3, B 3.** Open, Download, Save, Share, Night, Spelling and a reading control are visible in both variants at both sizes. Each download overlay has explicit Markdown and HTML choices and a close control. A's state-dependent “Back to editor” is more informative than B's unchanged “Reading” label, while B exposes both source and preview in normal phone mode. The supplied document remains recognizable in all shown document states. These observations establish discoverability only, not that any export, import, storage, link or shortcut actually works.

4. **Phone continuity and operation — A 3, B 2.** A devotes the area below the compact toolbar to the current task. In its phone document screenshot the source is visible through the long paragraph, quotation and beginning of the table; reading mode gives the preview the full available height with a visible “Back to editor” control. B keeps two approximately 360-pixel-high panes stacked: the source cuts off in the first long list item and the preview also cuts off within the long text. This makes the normal phone view substantially more fragmented for long writing/review, although B's Reading state remedies preview space. Both keep all commands within the viewport and both download dialogs fit. A's six densely packed second-row commands and B's small mode labels still leave room to improve touch clarity; actual hitboxes and scrolling were not tested.

5. **Desktop space and layout — A 3, B 3.** Both show a balanced side-by-side source and preview without obvious overflow at 1440×900, and both fit the whole sample document. Both center a roughly 840-pixel-wide preview in reading mode, preserving a manageable line length rather than stretching text across the display. B's margins and panel boundaries make work areas especially distinct; A uses more of the available workspace with less framing. Both are coherent choices supported by the screenshots.

6. **Consistency and restraint across states — A 3, B 3.** Both preserve their toolbar, document hierarchy and workspace relationships through initial, populated, reading, night and download states. Night screenshots maintain readable document text and visible active toggles. B carries its warm boundary and accent language into its light download dialog; A carries its green accent into active states and its dialog, though the dark dialog is a more abrupt change within the light workspace. These differences do not by themselves establish better task performance.

## Coverage

Viewed all 20 supplied PNGs individually at their original dimensions using the image viewer: A and B × 1440×900 and 390×844 × initial, document, reading, night and download. Read only the assigned request.txt and manifest.json in addition to these screenshots. Did not inspect source code, skills, group identities, budgets, prior results or other reviews. Did not use the optional black-box pages.

## Limitations

Static screenshots cannot verify live rendering behavior, scroll synchronization or scroll containment, touch target bounds, keyboard behavior, focus restoration/trapping, spell-check behavior, imports, downloads, browser save/reload or shareable-link correctness. Software keyboard effects and documents longer than the shown sample were not observed. Preservation of original implementation APIs, DOM IDs, vendor files and exact brand fidelity cannot be established from this evidence. The score advantage is deliberately narrow because the principal observed difference is phone workspace allocation, while desktop and reading layouts are similarly usable.
